About Japanese Writing Systems

Japanese uses three writing systems simultaneously:

  • Hiragana: Phonetic alphabet for native words
  • Katakana: Used for foreign words and emphasis
  • Kanji: Chinese-derived characters with meanings

Recommended learning order: Hiragana → Katakana → Kanji
