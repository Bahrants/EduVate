Taariikhda Af Soomaaliga
History of Somali Language
Af Soomaaligu waa afka ugu weyn ee qoomiyada Soomaaliyeed, kuwaas oo ku hadla Geeska Afrika.
Somali is the largest language of the Somali people, spoken in the Horn of Africa.
Qoraalka Soomaaliga waxaa la isticmaali jiray qoraal Faransiis ah illaa 1972-kii, markii la ansixiyay qoraalka Laatiinka.
The Somali script used French-based orthography until 1972 when the Latin alphabet was officially adopted.
Qoraalka cusub wuxuu sameeyay Shire Jaamac Axmed, isagoo ku salaysan qoraalka Laatiinka iyo qodobo gaar ah.
The modern script was developed by Shire Jama Ahmed using Latin letters with unique additions.
